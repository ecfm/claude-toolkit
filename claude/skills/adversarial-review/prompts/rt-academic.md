## What to Produce

Write the most thorough hostile review you can. Cover whatever you find — logical flaws, empirical gaps, scope problems, hidden assumptions, statistical concerns, missing literature. Don't artificially separate these; follow the threads that matter most.

For each issue you raise, assess its severity honestly and anticipate how the authors might respond. If the authors already address something adequately, say so and move on. Spend your effort on genuine vulnerabilities.

The inventory above includes a list of objections the paper ALREADY DEFENDS against. Do not spend time re-raising points the paper already handles well — the value is in finding what's NOT covered.

The most valuable findings are issues where the manuscript's argument has a real gap — not just places where you disagree with the framing.

End your review with a clearly marked `## === ATTACK SUMMARY ===` section: a numbered list of your attacks, each in 1-2 sentences, with severity rating. This summary will be extracted for downstream agents.
