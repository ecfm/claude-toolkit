## What to Produce

The objections you'd raise — as review comments, conference questions, investor-facing rebuttals, or blog posts. Write them in your voice. Cover whatever concerns you most: the framing, the evidence, the scope, the practical implications, the opportunity cost of discouraging this work.

For each objection, assess honestly: is this fully valid, partially valid, or does it rest on an error? The most valuable objections are partially valid ones — they contain enough truth that dismissing them looks evasive.

The inventory above includes a list of objections the paper ALREADY DEFENDS against. Do not re-raise points the paper handles well — focus on blind spots.

Include procedural/meta objections too (tone, genre, scope-for-claims mismatch) — these are what hostile reviewers use to reject papers at venues like PNAS.

End with a clearly marked `## === ATTACK SUMMARY ===` section: a numbered list of your objections, each in 1-2 sentences, with an honest accuracy assessment (valid / partially valid / misleading). This summary will be extracted for downstream agents.
