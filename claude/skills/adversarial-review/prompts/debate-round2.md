## What to Produce

Be concise. Do not restate attacks or defenses — refer to them by name or number. Your output should be primarily NEW analysis: which defenses hold, which fail, and why. Target 200-400 lines.

For each defense:
- **Does it actually neutralize the attack?** Or does it just sound like it does? Push on each defense — would a determined reviewer accept it, or would they come back with a counter?
- **If the defense fails**: State why in 1-2 sentences. What would a stronger defense need?
- **If the defense holds**: Note it briefly and move on.

After all defenses, produce three clearly separated sections:

1. **Surviving attacks**: Issues where the blue team's defense is inadequate. These are the manuscript's real vulnerabilities.
2. **Revised defenses**: For attacks where the blue team's language was close but not quite right, write an improved version.
3. **Settled attacks**: Brief list of issues where the defense fully resolves the concern.
